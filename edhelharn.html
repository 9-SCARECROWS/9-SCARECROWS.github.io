Aragorn II, son of Arathorn; called Edhelharn, Elessar, the Elfstone; Strider, Telcontar, Dúnadan; the heir of Isildur, Elendil's son of Gondor.
He is the sword that was broken and is forged again!

I would have followed you into the very fires of Mordor...

Aragorn is the shining example of the race of Men. Some of this goodness may come from his childhood among the Elves; but some of it, certainly, is a choice. He chooses, endlessly, to be a good man.

To be entirely honest, he completely blows other men out of the water. Aragorn raises the bar to a level that is nigh unreachable. It's bad enough that he's played by Viggo Mortensen, renowned sex symbol at the time (and ongoing silver fox nowadays). But on top of this, he spends the trilogy being admirable, kind, sarcastic, playful, and deeply noble.

But it is not this day! This day we fight!

What does your Heart tell you?

A collection of music that makes me think of Aragorn, for you to listen to while viewing the website.

Lay of Lúthien - Viggo Mortensen

"What happened to her?" "She died." Is there more to say...? Of course there is. The love of Beren and Lúthien is beautiful exactly because it is unending despite being inherently tied to a fleeting mortal life; this is also true of the evenstar love of Arwen and Aragorn.

Thirty-Three - Smashing Pumpkins

"I know I'll make it, love can last forever; Graceful swans of never topple to the earth."

It's Been Awhile - Staind

I like myself an angsty, yearning, Elrond-daddy-issues-having Aragorn Elfstone. So:
"I cannot blame this on my father / he did the best he could for me." Indeed, Elrond did everything he could for little Estel, raised him as his own son for many years... Of course, that only makes it hurt Aragorn worse when Elrond insists he doesn't belong with Arwen, or Elrond, or any of elfkind. But the song is very Aragorn overall, as well.

I Am the Highway - Audioslave

I think, as Strider, Aragorn would have felt particularly at home in nature. Not just in it, but of it, a wild thing himself. I also this fits how I imagine himself thinking of his semi-estrangement from his adopted Elven family, and his distance from Arwen.

How Soon is Now? - The Smiths

"I am the son, and the heir / Of nothing, in particular." I like this song for a particularly disillusioned Strider. He has no plans to become king, having forsaken his heritage; he can't imagine a reason to be proud of being Isildur's heir, and in fact finds that relation very shameful.

Mayonaise - Smashing Pumpkins

The entirety of the last verse and chorus. Aragorn spends his adolesence and early adulthood running. From Sauron, yes, but also from responsibility. Aragorn knows he will have to follow that path eventually; but he just wants to be a man, for a while. When he can, he will.

Like a Stone - Audioslave

Too emotional to write more than that Aragorn the Elfstone loves Arwen desperately.

Drive - Incubus

Aragorn is very self-driven and doesn't let fear control him. But we do see that is through effort and choice, given that he can despair and lose hope at times, and he chooses to keep going. Put water over wine and take the wheel.

Nutshell - Alice in Chains

I imagine that being told who he really is and leaving to go be Chieftan of the Dúnedain would have been devastating for young Estel. I have very big feelings about this period of his life, to the point of needing to write about it... the entire second verse makes me think of this. Loss of identity is very hard.

Dear Wormwood - The Oh Hellos

I've loved this song since I first heard it, and it's particularly painful to apply to Aragorn. I think the first verse is addressed to Elrond, but after that, I think I picture it as addressed kind of... to Sauron? And to the evils in the world, generally. This is Aragorn coming into himself, choosing his path, and choosing to be the Hope of Men.

i Vanda Elendilwa - Viggo Mortensen

Et Eärello Endorenna utúlien. Sinomë maruvan ar Hildinyar tenn' Ambar-metta.

Out of the Great Sea, to Middle-earth I am come. In this place will I abide, and my heirs, unto the ending of the world.
